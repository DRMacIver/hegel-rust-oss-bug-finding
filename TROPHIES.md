Triage bar for inclusion:
1. Reproduced by me independently of the subagent (minimal repro, pinned commit).
2. Verified against documented behavior — the library violates its own contract, not an assumption we invented.
3. Checked upstream issue tracker for duplicates.
4. Not one of the already-published blog examples (fraction, rust_decimal sci-notation, heck ß, im-rs get_prev).
